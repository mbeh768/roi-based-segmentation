    from claude:
    - extended with a crop-to-local auxiliary term: online crops feat1 at pos
      via _crop_and_resize to (crop_size, crop_size) and a throwaway local_head
      decodes that crop to pixel-space prediction of the downsampled local tile
      (pixel MSE). local tile is NEVER fed through the unet — only the cropped
      global features are trained against it. this keeps the encoder's input
      distribution matched to downstream (globals only).
    - crop_size semantics: it is the spatial size of
        (a) the crop taken from the global feature map at pos, and
        (b) the downsampled local tile that serves as the MSE target.
      downstream in PretrainedLocalGlobalUNet the cropped global features are
      resized to the local encoder's bottleneck spatial dim (local_tile / 16,
      i.e. 16 for 256px tiles) and concatenated before the decoder. setting
      crop_size = 16 makes the SSL pretext operate at the exact spatial
      granularity features will be consumed at downstream — features at each
      position in the 16x16 crop learn to encode the semantic content of that
      1/16 x 1/16 region of the tile, which is what the bottleneck decoder
      will read. mismatching crop_size against the bottleneck doesn't break
      anything (resize handles it) but wastes capacity training features at a
      resolution they're never used at.
