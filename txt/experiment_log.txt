

---- Maximum Improvment of Reconstruction-Based SSL ---

two branches
    global - reconstruction of the global image following the key augmentation of removing a large rectangular region from the image
    local - reconstruction of the actual local region based on global features




[EXPERIMENT 1] - establishing a baseline
baseline for performance of the self supervised learning-trained static global encoder

Results:

    [PROCESS 1A] - training
    SSL will be run in its most basic form, significant photometric augmentations, chunk masking, and 0.1 vallidation split with an early stop of 50 epochs


    [EXPERIMENT 1B] - performance of static encoder generated by baseline ssl
    an overfit will be performed on the supervised dataset with no augmentations,
    loss vs. iteration graph will be recorded

    Expected: strong overfit 
    Results:


    [EXPEIRMENT 1C] - performance of static encoder generated by baseline ssl
    an overfit will be performed on the supervised dataset with ONLY geometric augmentations
    these will include a horizontal and veritcal flip, and 90 degree rotation, see config for details

    Expected: more time required for overfit, but equally strong
    Results:


[EXPERIMENT 2] - intelligent loss computation
Noisy gradients in ssl slow convergence and reduce quality of latent space produced

    [PROCESS 2A] - training with targeted global loss
    SSL will be trained identically to PROCESS 1A, with only the reconstructed region being evaluated in the loss function, unaltered tiles are ignored


    [PROCESS 2B] - training with consistent local gradients
    SSL will be trained identically to PROCESS 1A, with the exception that backpropagation does not occur until all local tiles have been reconstructed, rather than a single one per image


    [EXPERIMENT 2C] - cooresponds to [PROCESS 2A]
    overfit without augmentation is performed with the static encoder as generated by [PROCESS 2A]

    Expected: earlier convergence than [EXPERIMENT 1B] due to a stronger emphasis on true understanding of reconstruction rather than compensation for photometric augmentation in the static global encoder
    Results:


    [EXPERIMENT 2D] - cooresponds to [PROCESS 2B]
    overfit without augmentation is performed with the static encoder as generated by [PROCESS 2B]

    Expected: eariler convergence than [EXPERIMENT 1B] due to a stronger locality of features enabled by a more clear and complete depiciton of the local loss landscape in training of the static global encdoer 
    Results:


    [EVALUATION 2E] - evaluate success of [EXPERIMENT 2C] and [EXPERIMENT 2D]
    should either of these experiments show a stronger overfit than [EXPERIMENT 1B], test their performance in the augmented overfit
