[EXPERIMENT 2] - intelligent global loss computation
Noisy gradients in ssl slow convergence and reduce quality of latent space produced

Results: weighting reconstruction 3x produces a very slighly superior overfit performance

NOTE: testing higher weights on reconstruction would be beneficial, did not test on overfit with augs

    [PROCESS 2A] - training with targeted global loss
    SSL will be trained identically to PROCESS 1B, with only the reconstructed region being evaluated in the loss function, unaltered tiles are ignored


    [PROCESS 2B] - focused global loss
    identical training scheme to [PROCESS 2A] but instead of not computing loss on unmasked tiles just weight the loss 3x on masked tiles


    [EXPERIMENT 2C] - cooresponds to [PROCESS 2A]
    overfit without augmentation is performed with the static encoder as generated by [PROCESS 2A]

    Expected: earlier convergence than [EXPERIMENT 1C] due to a stronger emphasis on true understanding of reconstruction rather than compensation for photometric augmentation in the static global encoder
    Results: later (~300 epochs behind) but equally strong overfit, slightly inferior to [EXPERIMENT 1C]


    [EXPERIMENT 2D] - cooresponds to [PROCESS 2B]
    overfit without augmentation (identical to [EXPERIMENT2C]) is performed with the static encoder as generated by [PROCESS 2B]

    Expected: stronger performance than [EXPERIMENT 2C] because other parts of image reconstruction also receive gradients, meaning more accurate photometric reversal
    Results: very interesting that a significant dip in iou happens in [EXPERIMENT 1C] and this experiment at epoch ~260, more severe in this one
    looking at tensorboard, i see very slightly more stable training with this scheme, going to proceed with it and note that testing with higher weights would be worthwhile
